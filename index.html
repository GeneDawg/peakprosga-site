Step 1 of 4 · How it works

Three steps. You run the sale. We handle the roof.

Peak Pros Georgia is your fulfillment team behind the scenes. The homeowner only ever deals with you.

01

You close the job

Sell Peak 301® roof rejuvenation to your customer under your own brand, at your own price. You control the margin.

02

Submit the assignment

Send the address and homeowner details from your partner dashboard. We measure the roof with EagleView and send you an estimate to e-sign. That signature is your work authorization.

03

We fulfill. You get paid.

A certified Peak 301® crew applies the treatment. You get the photos, the invoice at $85/sq and the homeowner's warranty. You keep everything above $85.

📊

Every partner gets a private dashboard. Submit jobs, see assignment and completion dates, job photos, invoices with pay links, payment status and completed warranties, all in one place and visible only to your company.

Questions

Frequently asked

Who actually does the work?

A certified Peak 301® application crew from our Georgia territory operator. We work as your subcontractor, so the homeowner relationship stays yours.

How is the job priced and billed?

You pay $85 per roofing square, measured by EagleView. Every job starts with an estimate you e-sign, and we invoice your company (not the homeowner) when the work is complete. Payment is due within 7 days, and you can pay online from the invoice or your dashboard.

What does the homeowner get?

A Peak 301® roof rejuvenation treatment and a 6-year transferable Peak 301® warranty. The completed warranty appears in your dashboard as soon as your invoice is paid, ready to hand to your customer.

What does it cost to join?

Nothing. There are no sign-up fees, monthly fees or minimums. You only pay for jobs you sell.

How do I get my access code?

Once your application is approved, we send your company its own private code. Use it at the Partner Login to reach your dashboard and submit jobs.

What areas do you cover?

Atlanta, Peachtree Corners, North Georgia and surrounding areas. Call us about jobs outside that area.
