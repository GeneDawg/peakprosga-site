The science

Roofs don't wear out all at once. They dry out.

Asphalt shingles age as sun and heat break down the asphalt that holds them together. Peak 301 is a soybean-based treatment that works inside the shingle, restoring the flexibility the roof has lost.

01 · Aging

Oils oxidize, shingles turn brittle

Over years of Georgia sun, the asphalt loses the oils that keep it pliable. Shingles crack, curl and shed the granules that protect them.

02 · Treatment

One spray-applied application

Certified crews apply Peak 301 across every shingle in a single visit. The soybean-based formula absorbs into the asphalt instead of sitting on top like a coating.

03 · Restored

Flexible, protected, warrantied

Shingles regain flexibility and hold their granules, so they can bounce back from weather. One application is backed by a 6-year transferable warranty.

Impact

Questions

Frequently asked.

What is Peak 301?

Peak 301® is a soybean-based rejuvenation treatment for asphalt shingle roofs, manufactured by Colorbiotics, part of the Sika family of companies. It's a 96% USDA Certified Biobased Product, applied in a single visit, and backed by a 6-year transferable warranty.

Which roofs are a good fit?

Asphalt shingle roofs that are aging but still structurally sound. It isn't for other roofing materials, and it isn't a substitute for repairs. Damaged areas should be repaired first.

Who actually does the work?

A certified Peak 301 application crew. We work as your subcontractor, so the homeowner relationship stays yours.

How is the job priced and billed?

You pay $85 per roofing square, measured by EagleView. Every job starts with an estimate you e-sign, and we invoice your company (not the homeowner) when the work is complete. Payment is due within 7 days, and you can pay online from the invoice or your dashboard.

When does the homeowner get the warranty?

The completed Peak 301 warranty appears in your dashboard as soon as your invoice is paid, ready to hand to your customer.

What does it cost to join?

Nothing. No sign-up fees, monthly fees or minimums. You only pay for jobs you sell.

How do I get my access code?

Once your application is approved, we send your company its own private code. Use it at Partner Login to reach your dashboard and submit jobs.

What areas do you cover?

Atlanta, Peachtree Corners, North Georgia and surrounding areas. Call us about jobs outside that area.
